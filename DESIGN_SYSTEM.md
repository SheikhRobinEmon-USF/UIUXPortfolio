# Design systems

The portfolio pairs Manrope with an italic editorial serif and Space Mono for indexes. Warm white and near-black frame seven full-bleed project previews. On desktop the split workspace makes selected imagery dominant and keeps a right-hand project index in view. On narrow screens the preview, project controls and selector stack. CSS perspective keeps nearby work visible. Reduced-motion mode removes transform animation, and native links preserve history and navigation.

## ATELIER

- **Brand and palette.** Considered design goods. ATELIER wordmark, warm canvas `#F8F2E7`, sunlit gallery `#DBAC7F`, espresso actions `#3B251C`.
- **Typography and grid.** Georgia display, Manrope text. Four-column collection, two-column product view and fixed label/price rhythm. 8–28 px gaps.
- **Components and navigation.** Editorial hero, product cards, gallery, material and size selectors, bag and order rows. Discover, Browse, Wishlist, Bag and Orders remain available.
- **Interaction and access.** Product image grows into detail where View Transitions are supported. Gallery, zoom and commerce controls remain operable through buttons and native fields. Smaller widths collapse the detail and checkout columns.

## COMMON

- **Brand and palette.** Small creative circles. COMMON wordmark, paper green `#F1F4E9`, acid green `#E5ED82`, forest `#233D2C`.
- **Typography and grid.** Manrope display and text. Split circle rail, content feed and context panel on wide screens. 8–24 px gaps.
- **Components and navigation.** Post, author avatar, circle, composer, comment, message and activity row. Feed, Explore, Communities, Saved, Messages, Notifications and Profile have distinct jobs.
- **Interaction and access.** The post and its image carry into detail. Composer and feedback work with native fields, visible reaction text and selected states. Mobile views stack the rails around the feed.

## FRAME

- **Brand and palette.** Editorial cinema. Spaced FRAME wordmark, near midnight `#0B0E14`, slate `#161E29`, cool blue `#A7C8DB`.
- **Typography and grid.** Manrope with large title scale and a restrained metadata treatment. Full-width hero, horizontally scrollable shelves and two-column title detail. 8–24 px gaps.
- **Components and navigation.** Film cards, genre filters, title and episode detail, viewer selection, watchlist and native media player.
- **Interaction and access.** Poster-to-title motion, native seek, volume, fullscreen and descriptive captions. Shelves support touch and keyboard. Viewer watch state is stored locally.

## VITAL

- **Brand and palette.** Low-pressure routines. VITAL wordmark, soft sage `#EAF0E5`, cream `#FFFEF7`, pine `#173D35`.
- **Typography and grid.** Friendly Manrope, open summary grid and rounded surfaces. 8–26 px gaps with a two-column detail area above the timeline.
- **Components and navigation.** Day, stat, dated log, chart, checklist, goal and journal. Today leads to dedicated movement, sleep, nutrition and habit flows.
- **Interaction and access.** Date changes refresh the timeline and per-unit bars. Units and totals are explicit. Native checkboxes and date fields are labeled, and the mobile dashboard stacks.

## MONETA

- **Brand and palette.** Clear financial context. MONETA wordmark, paper blue `#EDF1F9`, account ink `#172947`, chart cobalt `#3A5EAC`.
- **Typography and grid.** Manrope labels with Space Mono totals. Four metric columns, split breakdown and budget areas. 6–20 px gaps and a 4 px radius.
- **Components and navigation.** Spending filters, transactions, budgets, account cards, subscription rows, savings goals and transfer form.
- **Interaction and access.** Range/category filters update spending together. Currency totals and controls are text-labeled and keyboard operable. At narrow widths stat cards switch to two columns and ledger panels stack.

## CRAVE

- **Brand and palette.** Neighborhood delivery. CRAVE wordmark, warm `#FFF9EE`, orange `#F4A259`, chili `#841F10`.
- **Typography and grid.** Heavy Manrope headlines and rounded action controls. Image-led discovery, compact menus and vertical cart rows. 8–24 px gaps.
- **Components and navigation.** Restaurant cover, dish options, cart, sample address, checkout, tracking and orders. Cart and tracking remain direct destinations.
- **Interaction and access.** Meal options update price, and explicit simulated status events advance the tracker. Statuses use text and symbols. Order review stacks below the cart on narrow screens.

## ROAM

- **Brand and palette.** Distinctive slow stays. ROAM wordmark, limestone `#F6F2E7`, sand `#E2D8BF`, dark bark `#352D23`.
- **Typography and grid.** Georgia titles with Manrope controls. Split map/list on wide screens and a single mobile column. 8–20 px spacing with room around photography.
- **Components and navigation.** Guest and date fields, nightly-rate filter, map markers, property cards, gallery, booking summary, saved list and itinerary.
- **Interaction and access.** Button selection links map and card state, with keyboard focus to the corresponding card. Guests, dates, rate and fees are listed in text.

Icons are sparse and paired with accessible control names. Workflows use scoped CSS, native forms where practical, visible focus and reduced-motion alternatives. Portfolio imagery and FRAME clips were created for this project. Case-study screenshots were captured from the production export.
