# Product design research · November 2026

These patterns come from published usability research and first-party product documentation. The product examples are evidence of current conventions. Each demo applies the principles to its own design, data and constraints.

| Demo | Modern pattern | Applied in this revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ATELIER · E‑commerce** | Shoppers benefit from clear categories and variant selection, with likely fulfillment costs visible near the purchase decision. [Baymard product page benchmark](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux) · [Checkout usability research](https://baymard.com/research/checkout-usability) | Category chips and direct access to the full collection support browsing. The product gallery stays alongside material and size controls. Standard and express shipping costs appear before the bag. |
| **COMMON · Social** | Modern communities distinguish following from discovery, organize posts with topic labels, and offer intentional sorting. [Reddit recommendations](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/23511859482388-Reddit-s-Approach-to-Content-Recommendations) · [Post flair](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/15484545678996-Post-Flair) | Circles, follower activity and the feed have clear entry points. Members choose a post type when composing and sort the feed by latest or popular. |
| **FRAME · Streaming** | Current streaming discovery surfaces present synopsis and useful playback details up front, with previews and a route back into viewing progress. Netflix’s documentation describes a TV experience, so the original web demo adapts the hierarchy to mouse, keyboard and touch. [Netflix’s redesign](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/netflix-new-homepage-layout-user-guide) | The featured title includes a synopsis, episode count and user-initiated captioned preview. A resume shelf uses the viewer’s actual local episode progress. |
| **VITAL · Health** | Health summaries favor scannable highlights, categories and drill-down trends. [Apple Health data and trends](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iphe3d379c32/ios) | The dashboard separates movement progress, logging and habit check-ins. Selecting a day in the trend chart updates the dashboard to that day’s simulated health data. |
| **MONETA · Finance** | Financial dashboards present account balances and period-based spending, with budgets and editable transactions as follow-up workflows. [Monarch Money reports](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/21846787088916-Using-Reports) · [Monarch budgeting](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048883631-Creating-Your-Budget-in-Monarch) | Available funds, filtered spending and monthly budget remaining appear together. The category view and seven-day spending chart use the demo ledger, with a route into account details. |
| **CRAVE · Food** | Set delivery context early, distinguish fees from meal price, and show status once a purchase is underway. [DoorDash address controls](https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/How-do-I-change-my-delivery-address) · [Fees](https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/what-fees-do-i-pay?langue=en_US) · [Order tracking](https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/customer-where-is-my-order?ctry=US) | A saved demo delivery area carries into checkout. Cuisine chips, delivery estimates and fees support comparison. The active tracking milestone includes its own sample estimate. |
| **ROAM · Stays** | Start with destination, dates and guests. Support list/map comparison and show the cost for the selected stay early. [Airbnb listing search](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/252) · [Stays and total-price filtering](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/479) · [Date-based pricing](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1369) | An integrated trip search precedes results. Cards recalculate the total based on nights and the configured cleaning fee. The map and list continue to stay linked. |

The demos use local fixtures for inventory, locations, orders, viewing history, activity and finances. Product announcements and usability findings above inform the design decisions. This release includes functional browser checks and does not report usability testing with participants.
